StudentScope — Student Performance Analysis & Prediction System

An end-to-end Data Science & Machine Learning platform engineered to analyze student behavioral patterns, monitor academic metrics, and predict final academic achievements to prevent early dropouts.

395 Dataset Records
33 Student Attributes
4 ML Algorithms
86.5% Top R² Score

Project Objective

  • Provide educational institutions and tutors with intuitive visual analytics on student progress.
  • Identify at-risk students before final evaluations take place.
  • Quantify the impact of study hours, failures, and class attendance on student outcomes.
  • Deliver an interactive prediction system suitable for academic counseling and intervention.

Problem Statement

Educational dropouts and poor academic performance often stem from unaddressed early warning indicators such as mounting absences or low mid-term grades. Traditional evaluation methods react after grades are finalized. StudentScope addresses this by deploying machine learning algorithms that forecast final period scores early in the term.
